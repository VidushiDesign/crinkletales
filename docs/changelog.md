# Changelog

## v0.1 · round 1 (20 Nov 2026)
**Why:** test the core loop with friends, family and designer colleagues: tell a story, get a junk page, play with it, keep it.

**Shipped**
- Animated 3-step intro, then a prompt to make the first page
- Capture by typing or talking (browser speech), with live "spotted" chips
- Rule-based page maker: travel, everyday and feelings themes, 1 to 2 pages
- Editor: drag, rotate, resize, layer, duplicate, delete, undo/redo, text editing with 4 fonts, stickers, washi tape, photos, paper texture and colour, add pages
- Two page styles: Pop and Japandi
- Journal with a cover that opens, theme filters, entry view with the full story in a pocket
- One insight card (people, longest theme or mood) plus a "coming soon" preview
- Synthesised paper, tape and sticker sounds
- Responsive: bottom nav on phones, left rail on desktop

**Not yet:** AI page making, more styles, music, search, mood calendar, ask your journal, year wrap-up, cloud sync.
