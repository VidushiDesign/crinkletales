# Changelog

## v0.1 · round 1 (20 Nov 2026)
**Why:** test the core loop with friends, family and designer colleagues: tell a story, get a junk page, play with it, keep it.

**Shipped**
- Animated 3-step intro, then a prompt to make the first page
- Capture by typing or talking (browser speech), with live "spotted" chips
- Rule-based page maker: travel, everyday and feelings themes, 1 to 2 pages
- Editor: drag, rotate, resize, layer, duplicate, delete, undo/redo, text editing with 4 fonts, stickers, washi tape, photos, paper texture and colour, add pages
- Two page styles: Pop and Japandi
- Journal with a cover that opens, theme filters, entry view with the full story in a pocket
- One insight card (people, longest theme or mood) plus a "coming soon" preview
- Synthesised paper, tape and sticker sounds
- Responsive: bottom nav on phones, left rail on desktop

**Not yet:** AI page making, more styles, music, search, mood calendar, ask your journal, year wrap-up, cloud sync.

## v0.2 (20 Nov 2026)
**Why:** feedback after the first click-through: pages felt silent, rough days need an out, music belongs to memories.

- Every item has its own pick-up and drop sound (tape peel, ticket flick, polaroid slide, sticker pop, note rustle, name tag slap, tag flutter, stamp thunk, marker squeak); rotating ticks, resizing stretches
- Page-making plays an ASMR sequence: rustle, snip, tape, stickers, stamp, glue
- "Skip the page, just keep my words" for rough days; can be turned into a page later
- Entry music player outside the page: cassette with "now playing", YouTube/Spotify link or an app track, plays when the entry opens
- Background music system (waiting for tracks in public/music/)
- No focus ring on text fields

## v0.3 (20 Nov 2026)
**Why:** the desktop home felt plain; the first collage mockup (A) was too busy, so the calmer version (B) was picked.

- New home: greeting with a highlighter, today's page card with washi tape, "This week" mood stickers (tap a day to open it, + for today), a memory resurfacing (on this day, or a page from over a week ago, with a line from it)
- Desktop: 2 columns, straighter cards, two stickers
- Background music player: in the side rail on desktop, a strip above the nav on phones; hidden until tracks are added

## v0.4 (21 Nov 2026)
- Background music composed in code (no files, no licences): 3 pieces, slow morning (soft piano), cloud nap (dreamy), window seat (sunny), with pads, piano plucks, bass and faint vinyl crackle
- The same tracks can be picked as a memory's song
- Music pauses while you record, so the mic doesn't hear it; it also steps aside while an entry's own song plays
- The background strip hides on entries, and sheets now sit above it
